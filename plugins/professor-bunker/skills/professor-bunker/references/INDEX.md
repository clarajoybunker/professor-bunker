# Professor Bunker knowledge library
Select modules by the decision or uncertainty, not by artifact type alone. These are teaching cues, not mandatory requirements. References are bibliographic starting points, not verified live links; verify before quoting, and seek current primary sources for standards or changing claims. All examples are fictional.
| Module | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Problem framing and critical thinking](01-framing.md) | When the problem, assumptions or causal explanation is unclear. |
| [Design process and methodology](02-process.md) | When the designer needs to choose an approach or level of fidelity. |
| [Systems and service design](03-systems.md) | When effects, handoffs, institutions or dependencies extend beyond screens. |
| [Product strategy and business](04-strategy.md) | When value, adoption, incentives or sustainability are uncertain. |
| [Product completeness and prioritization](05-completeness.md) | When screens omit flows, dependencies or decisions about scope. |
| [Interaction design and HCI](06-interaction.md) | When users may struggle to understand, navigate or operate a design. |
| [Visual design and design systems](07-visual.md) | When hierarchy, composition, visual language or reusable structure matters. |
| [Psychology and behavioral economics](08-behavior.md) | When a proposal depends on motivation, judgment, trust or repeated behavior. |
| [Ethics, society and technology](09-ethics.md) | When benefits, harms, rights, data or authority are unevenly distributed. |
| [Evaluation and decision-making](10-evaluation.md) | When choosing between alternatives or deciding whether evidence is sufficient. |
| [Research and evidence](11-research.md) | When a designer needs evidence to understand a situation or evaluate a proposal. |
| [Project planning, scope and complexity](12-planning.md) | When sequencing work, calibrating effort, identifying project risks, or expanding and contracting scope. |
| [Testing and experimentation](13-experimentation.md) | When choosing testing methods, comparing alternatives, planning live experiments or finding feasible early signals. |
| [Source quality and verification](14-sourcing.md) | Mandatory for externally grounded claims and teaching concepts; choose and verify reputable sources. |
