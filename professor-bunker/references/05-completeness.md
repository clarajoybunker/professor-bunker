# Product completeness and prioritization
When screens omit flows, dependencies or decisions about scope.

## Concepts and application cues

- **MVP / North Star:** Separate a coherent minimum experiment or product from the longer-term vision; minimum does not excuse unsafe or unusable functionality.
- **Feature dependencies:** Map prerequisites and outputs across features; distinguish required dependencies from nice-to-haves.
- **End-to-end flow / lifecycle:** Include discovery, entry, setup, core action, completion, follow-up and removal where relevant.
- **Happy path / edge cases:** Explore empty, loading, error, offline, partial success and recovery states according to likelihood and consequence.
- **CRUD:** Consider create, read, update and delete for relevant entities; not every entity needs all four exposed to every role.
- **State transitions:** Define states, triggering events, guard conditions and outcomes; check back, cancel, undo and retries.
- **Permissions / roles:** Establish who can see, create, change, approve or remove resources; use least privilege.
- **Kano model:** Explore expected, performance and delight attributes; categories change by audience and over time.
- **RICE / ICE / impact-effort:** Compare candidate work with explicit estimates, uncertainty and rationale; do not mistake numerical rankings for evidence.
- **Opportunity cost:** Explain what cannot be done if a feature is added; prioritize the highest-value uncertainty or dependency.

## Fictional example
A fictional shared workshop booking app has a reserve screen but no cancellation, capacity conflict or confirmation. Map the minimum usable flow before adding recommendations.

## Limits and misuse
Completeness is proportional to the project goal: a concept test need not implement every production state. Never sacrifice critical safety or accessibility for scope.

## Teach it
Name the relevant concept, explain why it helps with the immediate decision, offer to walk through the method, and use a small example. Ask what would count as useful completion. Do not assign a framework merely because it exists.
